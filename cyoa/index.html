Path 01 of 14 · The Forwardable Manager

You manage × leverage

GDPval anchor: more benchmark occupations are yours than anyone's — general & operations managers, administrative services managers, project management specialists, every first-line supervisor. The record of valuable work in your function is public.
01 · The Rate MemoSHIP: a one-page memo pricing your role against current market ranges, written so your boss forwards it unedited.WHY: the ask is the highest-leverage document most people never write (Babcock). GDPval: managers are tested on forwardable decision docs.MOVE: the week after a visible win. Subject: "For your next comp conversation." Then stop talking.
02 · The Succession DocSHIP: the two-page "how my area actually runs" — structured so someone could cover you for two weeks.WHY: indispensable people get trapped; promotable people build systems. GDPval: admin-services managers are scored on operations documentation.MOVE: hand it over before review season: "This is what it takes to run my area. Let's talk about what I run next."
03 · The Headcount CaseSHIP: the numbers-and-risk memo: hours in vs. available, cost of the gap, two options priced.WHY: "we're drowning" is a feeling; leadership buys math. GDPval: supervisor tasks ARE staffing math — coverage, lines, recovery plans.MOVE: deliver when the pain is visible to others — post-crunch, post-turnover — not at your angriest.
04 · The Unsolicited One-PagerSHIP: the fix nobody assigned you: problem → cost → fix → what you need → 30-day result.WHY: demonstration beats permission (Godin). GDPval: PM specialists score on exactly this — the process doc that standardizes chaos.MOVE: give it to the person whose problem it solves. The best sponsor is the person you just saved.
05 · The Translate-Up BriefSHIP: a quarterly half-pager converting your team's work into outcomes, money, and risk retired.WHY: sponsors advocate for people who make advocacy easy (Hewlett). GDPval: reporting leadership can act on is the measured skill.MOVE: quarterly, unasked, same format. By the third one it's an institution — and so are you.

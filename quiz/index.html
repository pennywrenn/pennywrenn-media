I used to manipulate search results for a living. White hat — but that's not the point.

The point is what the job taught me: nothing on the internet is about good or bad, deserving or undeserving. It's about how things are packaged — and for twenty years, packaging took a team you couldn't afford and hours you didn't have.

Then the machine showed up. I've watched it collapse the distance between knowing better and doing better — in my own work, on a public livestream, with receipts. The raise case that never got written. The business that never got named. The pivot that never got tested. The bottleneck was never your judgment. It was throughput. That's over.
